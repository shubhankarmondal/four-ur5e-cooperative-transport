## 3. Files derived from the models above

- `models/four_ur5e_transport.xml` is generated by `src/homtrans/scene.py`. It
  contains modified copies of the MJCF content of both models above, including
  their body trees, inertial properties, geoms, materials and defaults. The code
  modifies that content when it builds the model: it replaces the UR5e's
  position servos with torque actuators, adds joint damping, removes the UR5e
  light, and adds name prefixes to the elements, renaming some of them. The file
  also refers to the meshes in `assets/`. The UR5e-derived parts of this file
  remain under BSD-3-Clause (Section 1), and the 2F-85-derived parts remain under
  BSD-2-Clause (Section 2). The skybox and checker-floor texture and material
  settings reuse the values in Menagerie's `universal_robots_ur5e/scene.xml`,
  which is covered by the BSD-3-Clause license in Section 1. This project wrote
  the payload, the plate handles, the stand, the lights and the cameras, and
  those parts are under MIT.
- The images and video in `media/` are renderings of these models.
